When can I download Bakasana?+

The iOS beta is available through Apple TestFlight. Android testers can join through Google Play on Android or join on the web.

Which devices are supported?+

The current iPhone beta is distributed through TestFlight, and the Android beta through Google Play. Final compatibility details will accompany the public release.

Do I need to create an account?+

No separate Bakasana account is required. You can use Guest Mode, or sign in with Game Center or Play Games for cloud progress and leaderboards.

Does Practice Timeline upload my camera clips?+

No. Camera access is optional, recordings are silent, and Practice Timeline media is processed and stored on your device.

How do I delete my data?+

Local data can be removed by uninstalling the app. Signed-in progress and platform data require additional steps described on our account deletion page.
