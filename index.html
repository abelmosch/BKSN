05 / FAQ

Clear answers about what Bakasana is.

Need help with the app, accounts, or Practice Timeline? Visit Support or email bakasana.app@gmail.com.

Is Bakasana a mobile app or a website?+

Bakasana is a mobile app for iPhone and Android. This website explains the product, practice approach, and support; the practice itself happens in the app.

Does Bakasana create yoga sequences?+

No. Bakasana is not a sequence builder or generator. You choose one of four programs and practice with a timer, guided voice, or free-form recording.

Does Bakasana replace a yoga teacher?+

No. Bakasana is a companion for regular practice and progress tracking. It does not replace a teacher, personalized instruction, or medical advice.
