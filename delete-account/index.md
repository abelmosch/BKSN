# Delete your Bakasana account and data

<p class="meta"><strong>Last updated:</strong> June 16, 2026<br><strong>App:</strong> Bakasana (iOS and Android)</p>

Bakasana does **not** operate separate username/password accounts. When you sign in, the App uses your **Apple Game Center** (iOS) or **Google Play Games** (Android) identity. **Guest Mode** stores data only on your device.

This page explains how to delete data linked to your use of Bakasana.

---

## Option 1 — Delete everything on this device (fastest)

1. Open **Settings** on your phone.
2. Find **Bakasana** in the app list.
3. Tap **Delete App** / **Uninstall**.

**Deleted immediately on the device:**

- Practice progress, streaks, and preferences stored locally
- Optional body weight
- Transformation Film frames and videos (if enabled)
- Legal acceptance and session preferences stored by the App

**Not deleted by uninstall alone:**

- Cloud progress saved in Game Center or Play Games (if you previously signed in)
- Leaderboard scores on Game Center / Play Games
- Analytics and crash diagnostics already sent to Firebase (Google)

---

## Option 2 — Signed-in users: remove cloud progress and platform data

### Android (Google Play Games)

1. In Bakasana, open **Profile** and tap **Sign out** (or delete the App after this step).
2. Open the **Google Play Games** app (or **Google Play** → **Play Games** → your profile).
3. Go to **Settings** → **Delete Play Games account & data** *or* manage **Saved games** for Bakasana and remove saved data associated with this app.
4. To remove leaderboard participation, manage your Play Games profile and app permissions in your **Google Account** settings.

Alternatively, email us (Option 3) with your **Play Games player ID** if shown in Profile, and we will guide you through removal where we can assist.

### iOS (Apple Game Center)

1. In Bakasana, open **Profile** and sign out, or uninstall the App.
2. On your iPhone, open **Settings** → **Game Center**.
3. Manage your Game Center account or sign out of Game Center on this device.
4. To remove Bakasana saved games, you may need to delete Bakasana cloud save data from **Settings** → **Apple ID** → **iCloud** → **Saved Data** (wording may vary by iOS version), or contact us with your Game Center player details.

---

## Option 3 — Request deletion by email (analytics & support)

Email **bakasana.app@gmail.com** with the subject **Bakasana account deletion request**.

Include:

- Platform (**iOS** or **Android**)
- Your **Game Center** or **Play Games display name** (if you signed in)
- Your **player ID** (shown in Bakasana Profile when signed in, if available)
- Whether you used **Guest Mode** only

We will respond within **30 days** and delete or anonymize identifiable analytics/crash records where technically feasible.

---

## What we delete vs. what may be retained

| Data | Deleted when | Notes |
|------|--------------|-------|
| Local practice progress & settings | Uninstall app | Immediate on device |
| Body weight (optional) | Uninstall app | Never uploaded to Bakasana servers |
| Transformation Film media | Uninstall app or disable/delete in Profile | Stored on device only |
| Play Games / Game Center saved progress | You remove via platform settings or our email request | Stored by Apple/Google |
| Leaderboard scores | Managed by Apple/Google platform policies | May persist until removed in platform settings |
| Firebase Analytics events | Email request + Google's retention cycle | Default retention ~**14 months** (Google) |
| Firebase Crashlytics reports | Email request + Google's retention cycle | Default retention ~**90 days** (Google) |

We do **not** maintain a separate Bakasana user database or server-side account store.

## Guest Mode users

If you only used **Continue without account**:

- Uninstalling Bakasana removes all App data from your device.
- Email us if you want help confirming that no platform account was linked.
